Search syntax
  • is:unread / is:read / is:starred — by status
  • feed:name / category:name — by source (fuzzy, case-insensitive)
  • title:word / author:name — by field
  • before:2026-01-01 / after:2026-01-01 — by date (UTC)
  • AND OR NOT and ( ) — combine; adjacent words imply AND
  • -term — exclude; "exact phrase" — quote (also for values with spaces, e.g. feed:"Rust Blog")
