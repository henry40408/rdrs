How “Fetched” and “Updated” are decided
  • Fetched — when rdrs last tried to read this feed, whether or not it worked. Every feed is retried about once an hour.
  • Updated — the newest of three signals, whichever of them this feed provides. They are not ranked: a missing signal is skipped, not treated as old.
  • Those signals are the feed’s own date (<updated>, falling back to <pubDate> / <lastBuildDate>), its newest entry’s date (<published>, falling back to that entry’s <updated>), and the Last-Modified header its server sends.
  • rdrs re-asks with If-None-Match / If-Modified-Since. A 304 Not Modified moves Fetched forward and leaves Updated untouched.
  • A feed that publishes no dates at all shows No date info; its age is then judged by Fetched instead.
  • Age: up to {{ fresh_max_days }} days is normal, {{ fresh_max_days + 1 }}–{{ warning_max_days }} days is a warning, over {{ warning_max_days }} days — or no date at all — is stale. The Stale filter matches only that last group.
  • A failed fetch shows its error under the row and counts towards the Errors filter; it clears as soon as one fetch succeeds. Feeds are never auto-disabled for failing.
